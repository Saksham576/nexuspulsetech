(05) FAQ

Questions.

Are the films on this page for real clients?

No. They are concept films for fictional brands, made to show our range and process. We say so clearly because trust matters more than looking big. Your project could be our first public case study.

Do you use AI?

We animate in code (HTML and WebGL rendered to video) and use AI tools for drafts and exploration. Every frame is designed and directed by a person. No stock templates.

What do you need from us?

Your website, logo, brand colours and fonts if you have them, and access to the product (or screenshots). Tell us your launch date and we'll plan backwards from it.

What about music and voiceover?

Every film ships with properly licensed or original music and sound design. Voiceover is available on the Signature plan.

How do payment and revisions work?

50% to start, 50% on delivery. International payments via PayPal or Wise; Indian clients via UPI or bank transfer. Two rounds of revisions are included.

Which time zones do you work with?

We're based in India and overlap with European days and US mornings. Most communication happens async over email.
